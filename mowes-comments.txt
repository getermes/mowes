MOWES — Comments to Post
=========================

HOW TO USE:
1. Open the video/post named below.
2. Tap the comment box ("Add a comment…").
3. Copy the comment under it and paste.
4. Tap Post.
Tip: don't paste a link (platforms hide those). Space comments a few minutes apart.


------------------------------------------------------------
YOUTUBE — LenaLifts
Video: "how to start working out: tips to be consistent, motivation & gym anxiety" (~1.2M views)
Link: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hxwd-9p54c8
------------------------------------------------------------

Loved the "create a realistic schedule" part. What finally worked for me after back pain forced me to train at home:

1. Start with just 3 days a week, not 7 — easy to keep, easy to win.
2. Don't start from a blank page — the hardest part is figuring out what to even do.
3. Check each workout off — that little momentum gets addicting.

That last problem is why I built a tiny free app called MOWES — it creates a full week of workouts for you, then you just adjust it to fit your body and your days. No more guessing, you just start and keep moving forward 🙏
