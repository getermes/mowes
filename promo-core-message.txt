MOWES — Promo Core Message ("incoming into training")
The shared heart of every promo. Two cuts (older + younger) build from THIS. Saved 2026-08-10.

======================================================================
HOOK LINE
======================================================================
Starting is the hardest part — MOWES walks you through it.

======================================================================
THE HEART (the "why")
======================================================================
Starting to move again is hard — where do you even begin? MOWES is a FREE workout
guide that takes you by the hand and walks you through it, one move at a time, right
on your phone. No gym. No sign-up. Nobody watching — your information never leaves
your phone. Whatever your age or shape: if you can tap a phone, you can start today.

======================================================================
THE 5 PROMISES (both cuts always say these)
======================================================================
- Totally free — no subscription, no catch
- Truly private — no sign-up, no tracking, stays on your phone
- Step-by-step — it guides every move; you're never lost
- No gym — just you and your phone, anywhere
- No shame — any age, any shape, built for starting from zero

======================================================================
FOUNDER LINE (powerful for video)
======================================================================
"I built MOWES because I needed it myself."  (Eugenio's real story = best promo.)

======================================================================
THE TWO CUTS (same heart, different emphasis)
======================================================================
OLDER cut  -> calm, hand-holding; lead on "if you can tap a phone, you can start."
              Slower/bigger. Show the real finger tapping the phone. Facebook / YouTube.
YOUNGER cut -> fast, punchy, privacy-forward; lead on "free, private, no tracking, your
              way." Quick cuts, energetic. TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts.

TWO MARKETS (reference)
  Older:   low tech, overweight-friendly, health-maintenance goal, free, reassuring.
  Younger: higher tech, overweight-friendly, no-shame/start-from-zero, free, energetic.
