MOWES — FEEDBACK & POLLS
Ways to hear from users (what works / what doesn't) without breaking the privacy promise.
Last updated: 2026-08-06

PRIVACY NOTE (important):
Feedback is ALWAYS opt-in. The app never sends anything on its own. When someone
taps "Send feedback" it opens THEIR email with a message pre-typed — they choose to
send it. So you get feedback AND keep the promise. Lead with this when you ask.

=========================================================
FEEDBACK-INVITING COMMENT  (post + pin on TikTok / YouTube)
=========================================================
I built MOWES to fix my *own* problem — starting a workout without overthinking it.
It sets up in seconds and walks you through step by step. 🔒 No sign-up, no account —
everything stays on your phone, nothing is ever collected or sent anywhere. But I can't
make it better without you: tell me what works, what doesn't, what's missing. I read
every comment, and I build this for you. 💪 getermes.github.io/mowes

=========================================================
POLL #1 — THE STRUGGLE  (best; feeds the mission)
=========================================================
Caption (in Eugenio's voice): I need your help 🙏 What's the #1 thing that stops you
from working out? 👇 I'm shaping MOWES around YOUR answers — a free app that guides
your whole workout, step by step. No sign-up, nothing leaves your phone.
getermes.github.io/mowes
Options:
  1) No time
  2) Don't know what to do
  3) No motivation
  4) Can't get to a gym

How to run it:
- TikTok: ask people to reply with their number, or add a Poll sticker on a photo post.
- YouTube: Community tab -> Poll (add the 4 options), or pin it as a comment.

=========================================================
POLL #2 — ABOUT THE APP  (use later)
=========================================================
If you tried MOWES, what would help you most?
  1) Reminders to work out
  2) More exercises to choose from
  3) Seeing my progress
  4) Even simpler setup
Caption: Tell me your number — it decides what I build next. 🙏
