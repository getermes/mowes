MOWES — Talking With Users
==========================
Your simple system: LISTEN -> TRACK -> FOLLOW UP.
- People reach you by replying in comments, or tapping "💬 Send feedback" in the app (goes to get.ermes00@gmail.com).
- Copy a ready reply below, tweak the name, post it.
- Log every bug/idea in the list at the bottom so nothing gets lost.
- When you fix or add something, go back and tell that person — it turns them into a fan.
