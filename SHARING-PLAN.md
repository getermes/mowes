# MOWES — Sharing Plan

A simple plan for getting your first real views by sharing the live YouTube
Short. Start with the easy wins (friends/family), then go wider.
Last updated: 2026-06-09.

- **Live Short:** https://youtube.com/shorts/ygt9Qml94N0
- **Live app:** https://getermes.github.io/mowes/

---

## Round 1 — People who know you (do this first)
Fastest views and warmest reactions.

**WhatsApp / iMessage to family & friends** — paste this:
> I built a free workout app to help your mom (Eugenio's wife). No sign-up,
> works on your phone even offline. Here's a quick 60-sec video — would love
> if you'd watch and tap like.
> https://youtube.com/shorts/ygt9Qml94N0

**Facebook (personal post):**
> I made something. It's called MOWES — a free, private workout planner you
> use right from your phone. No sign-up, no account, nothing tracked. I built
> it to replace the pen-and-paper workout sheets my wife kept losing. Here's a
> short video. Try it free: https://getermes.github.io/mowes/
> https://youtube.com/shorts/ygt9Qml94N0

---

## Round 2 — Wider audience (over the next few days)
- **Instagram** — post the Short as a Reel, same caption as Facebook.
- **WhatsApp Status / FB Story** — quick "Check out my new free app" + link.
- **Reddit** — communities like r/fitness, r/bodyweightfitness, r/selfhosted
  (they love private/offline apps). Be genuine, tell the story, don't just
  drop a link.

---

## The one rule that makes sharing work
Always lead with the **story and the privacy promise**, not the features:
> "No sign-up, no account, no email. Your data stays on your phone — it's
> never sent anywhere. Free, and works offline."
