Saved 2026-08-07.

=========================================================
THE CORE PITCH
=========================================================
Whether you have a platform or not, MOWES helps you reach and coach people.

Two doors for trainers:
1) NO APP? → MOWES is your app. Build your classes/program in MOWES, publish it,
   and your clients follow it — guided, step by step, on their own phones.
2) ALREADY HAVE CLASSES? → MOWES is exposure. Get in front of motivated beginners
   and send them to your own channel/classes.

=========================================================
READY-TO-USE LINES
=========================================================
- No app? MOWES is your app. Build your program, publish it — your clients follow it, guided, on their phones. 💪
- Already coaching online? Get discovered by motivated beginners. List your program on MOWES — you keep your members and your money. 🔒
- Trainers: reach people who WANT to start. Publish your classes on MOWES, or link out to your own — either way, you're in front of them.
- You bring the coaching. MOWES brings the tools, the guidance, and the audience.

=========================================================
WHY IT'S SAFE / ON-BRAND
=========================================================
- Trainer keeps their membership + payment on THEIR side. MOWES takes no cut of the user's payment.
- No accounts, nothing leaves the user's phone — the Eugenio privacy promise stays intact. 🔒
- Programs can be shared by link/code (like Backup/Restore) — no server needed.
