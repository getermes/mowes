# TOWES — Trainer Approval Rules

The checklist for deciding which trainers get a card in the MOWES/TOWES Trainers hub.
Used by the "review desk" flow: sign-ups come in → each is checked against these rules →
a YES/NO recommendation is given → Eugenio gives the final approval → welcome reply sent + card added.

## The one question that matters most
**"Would I hand this coach to my mom if she were a nervous beginner?"**
- Yes → add the card.
- Hesitate → don't. When in doubt, leave it out. Our everyday users are trusting us.

## The trick
The form asks for the trainer's **link** (YouTube / Instagram / TikTok / site).
A real trainer has a public presence, so their link shows their actual work in ~2 minutes.
No link, dead link, or private account → easy no.

## 🚦 Green lights (lean YES)
- Real, active link with real content (videos/posts of them actually coaching)
- Advice looks safe and beginner-friendly — good form, sensible progressions
- Warm, encouraging tone (fits our "never shame" brand)
- Some social proof — followers, comments, people they've helped

## 🚩 Red flags (lean NO)
- No link, private account, or link that's just a sales page
- Wild promises — "lose 30 lbs in 2 weeks," "secret trick"
- Pushing pills / supplements / MLM more than actual training
- Risky or ego-lifting advice that could hurt a beginner
- Rude, shaming, or "no pain no gain" bullying tone

## Nice touch
When adding a trainer, reply "welcome aboard." The relationship is worth a lot early on.
