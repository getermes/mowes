=========================================================
THE MISSION (the WHY behind every app)
=========================================================
"When I see a problem, I find a way to overcome it. Then I ask whether
it hurts others too — and if it does, I build the solution to help them."

Ways to say it:
- Short (tagline):  "I solve my own problems — then share the fix with you."
- Warm (videos/about): "I build these because I hit the problem myself. And I
  figured — if it bugged me, it probably bugs you too. So here's the fix."
- One-liner for posts: "Made by someone who had the same problem you do."

Why it matters: people don't fall in love with apps — they fall in love with
a person who genuinely wanted to help them. This is the most trustworthy thing
we can tell people, and it makes the privacy promise even stronger: not a
company chasing data, just one person solving real problems.

=========================================================
WHAT EVERY EUGENIO APP STANDS FOR
=========================================================
🔒 Private      — Your data stays on your device. No tracking, no selling, no spying.
🛡️ Secure       — No accounts to hack, no passwords to steal, nothing to leak.
🙂 Friendly     — Warm, simple, and welcoming. Built for real people.
👆 Intuitive    — If you can use a phone, you can use it. No manual needed.
✨ Unique       — Made with a fresh idea, not a copy of everyone else.
💡 Problem solver — It exists to make one real thing in your life easier.
